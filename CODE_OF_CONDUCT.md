# Code of conduct

SDBX contributors, maintainers, and users must help keep project spaces
professional, safe, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work, evidence, and impact rather than attacking a person.
- Be direct without harassment, humiliation, threats, or discriminatory
  language.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's identity, location,
  credentials, private messages, deployment data, or security report.
- Accept technical correction and review decisions without retaliation.
- Give newcomers enough context to participate without lowering security or
  quality standards.
- Stop unwanted personal contact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, stalking, threats, intimidation, or sexualized attention;
- discrimination based on identity, background, ability, or experience;
- doxxing or publishing private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, or coordinated abuse;
- using security disclosures, access, or project data to pressure a person;
- encouraging unlawful access to systems or content;
- retaliation against a good-faith report.

Technical disagreement, a rejected proposal, or a firm review is not by itself
harassment. The manner and pattern of conduct still matter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, restrict
participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant.
Actions should be proportionate to severity, pattern, risk, and the safety of
affected people.

Reports are handled confidentially on a need-to-know basis where possible.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed; an uninvolved maintainer should
handle the report. No one will be promised anonymity that cannot actually be
preserved by the communication platform or legal process.

Send a private conduct report to the project lead using the public contact on
[Maiko's GitHub profile](https://github.com/maiko). Include links or screenshots
needed to understand the report, but do not send credentials or unrelated
private deployment data. For abuse that also violates GitHub's platform rules,
use GitHub's own content-reporting tools as well. Do not infer a working mailbox
from the SDBX project domain.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, release
channels, project-operated communication spaces, and when a person represents
SDBX in a public project context.

## Appeals

A person subject to an enforcement action may request one review by an
uninvolved maintainer. The review considers process, conflicts of interest,
and proportionality; it is not an opportunity to repeat abuse or expose the
reporter.
